Privacy Policy

Nerite · Last updated 13 September 2026

Nerite is an aquarium logbook. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, what it does not, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version

Everything you record about your aquariums stays on your device. Tanks, water test results, dosing history, livestock, health observations, equipment, food and reminders are stored in a local database on your phone. We never receive them, and there is no account to create.

What is collected

DataWhyWho receives it
Crash reports and diagnostics To find and fix crashes Google (Firebase Crashlytics)
Anonymous usage events — screens opened, features used To understand which features are worth keeping Google (Firebase Analytics)
A device notification token Only if you enable notifications, so reminders and occasional tips can be delivered Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
Purchase receipts To verify a subscription or lifetime unlock and restore it on your other devices Google Play or Apple, and our server
Advertising identifier and approximate location derived from your IP address To show ads in the free version Google AdMob and its advertising partners

An anonymous identifier is created so a purchase can be tied to a device without asking for your name or email. It is not linked to any real-world identity.

What is never collected

  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Your aquarium data — readings, livestock, notes, photographs
  • Precise GPS location
  • Your contacts, calendar, microphone or camera roll

Advertising and your consent

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app presents a Google User Messaging Platform consent form the first time it runs, and asks for your choice before any ad is requested. You can change that choice at any time from Settings › Ad privacy options.

Buying Premium removes all advertising, and with it the advertising identifier collection.

Children

Nerite is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Ads are limited to G-rated content.

Your rights

Because your aquarium data never leaves your device, you control it directly:

  • Delete everything — Settings › Erase all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Stop analytics and crash reporting — use your device's system setting to opt out of the advertising identifier, or contact us to request deletion of the diagnostic data associated with your installation.
  • Access, correction, erasure and portability — if you are covered by the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA, write to us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

We do not sell personal information, and we never have.

Retention

Diagnostic and analytics data is retained by Google for up to 14 months. Purchase receipts are retained for as long as you hold the entitlement plus the period required by tax law. Device notification tokens are deleted when you disable notifications or uninstall.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects what is collected, tell you inside the app before it takes effect.
